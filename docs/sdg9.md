# SolvIQ and Sustainable Development Goal 9

SolvIQ's contribution is an accessible, traceable analytical workflow for first-pass corporate credit screening. It relates indirectly to [UN SDG 9 and target 9.3](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9), which addresses small enterprises' access to financial services and markets.

- **Accessibility.** The public pathway uses free SEC EDGAR data where covered. The workflow also allows analysts to supply and confirm financial statements where public data is unavailable.
- **Financial innovation.** A common workflow links confirmed inputs, deterministic ratios, alerts, charts and a portable report. Smaller teams can evaluate it without buying a data feed for the supported public pathway.
- **Transparency and reliability.** The output labels periods, currencies and unavailable values, exposes formulas and records source references where supplied.
- **Responsible AI.** The rule-based commentary runs locally and distinguishes observations from interpretations. Judgment and lending decisions remain with professionals.
- **Limitations.** The prototype does not establish improved access to lending. Its public pathway is limited to supported filers and the scoring methodology is designed for non-financial corporates. PDF inputs and incomplete data can still require manual review.

A pilot can measure access to traceable core figures, onboarding time, reviewer rework and disclosure of unavailable figures. Credit outcomes would need a separate study.
